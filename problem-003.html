The Problem

Find all integer solutions to the equation 2x^2 + 5y^2 = 7z^2, given that x, y, z are positive integers with gcd(x, y, z) = 1. Focus on parity and modular constraints.

Provide a proof that explains why no other primitive triples exist and describe the permitted families of solutions.

(a)
Part (a)

Parity Analysis

Show that one of x, y, z must be even and deduce constraints modulo 4 and modulo 7.

(b)
Part (b)

Primitive Solutions

Assuming gcd(x, y, z) = 1, determine the smallest nontrivial solution and justify why it is minimal.

(c)
Part (c)

Solution Families

Describe all solutions that arise by scaling the primitive triple and explain why these are the only families.
