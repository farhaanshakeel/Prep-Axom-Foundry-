// 01 — About

What is PAF?

Prep Axiom Foundry is a peer-led academic guild for students preparing for olympiad mathematics, physics, and advanced science. It is built by students, for students.

Members learn in focused study groups, solve exceptional problems, and take moderated tests while staying supported by mentors and an advocate.

We value deep reasoning more than speed, consistency more than flash, and wellbeing more than burnout.

“This is not a tutoring platform. It is a place where serious students shape their own understanding together.” — Auvalen, Founder
